Notable

A feedback-first livestream engagement tool where viewers send a required emoji reaction and optional message without paying, then may optionally attach a Mayar QRIS tip. Free reactions create lightweight, ephemeral OBS feedback; confirmed tips retain the reliable celebration flow. The approved pivot builds incrementally on the existing single-streamer application.

Elixir 1.18Phoenix 1.8 LiveViewSQLiteTailwindPhoenix PubSubExUnit · PhoenixTest · Credo · Dialyxir

What the app does

  • Feedback submission

    Viewers send a name, one Bad/Okay/Good/Great reaction, and an optional message for free.

  • Optional appreciation

    A collapsed toggle reveals IDR presets or a custom amount and Mayar QRIS checkout.

  • Unified OBS overlay

    Free reactions float simultaneously; confirmed tips use the sequential, recoverable celebration.

  • Proportionate spam protection

    Each IP may send one accepted free Note per 10 seconds; tipped Notes are exempt.

  • Unified admin inbox

    The streamer filters newest-first Notes by All, Tips, or Feedback and replays paid tips.

  • Notable public experience

    The canonical mobile-first experience lives at /, with /donate redirecting there.

Already provided by the existing codebase

  • Responsive donation form
  • Pending persistence and QR
  • Deduplicated webhooks
  • Sequential recoverable celebrations
  • Basic-auth live admin and replay
  • Environment-based deployment setup

Out of scope (v1)

  • Accounts and scale — No viewer accounts, multi-streamer, or team support.
  • Expanded customization — No extra payment methods, custom reactions, themes, logo package, custom domain, marketing site, or localization.
  • Moderation and identity controls — No moderation beyond rate limiting, CAPTCHA, bans, fingerprinting, filtering, or IP display/configuration.
  • Engagement expansion — No viewer history, analytics, gamification, livestream APIs, mobile app, or extensions.
  • Free-reaction recovery — Free feedback is intentionally ephemeral and is never replayed after an overlay restart.
  • Internal rename — Donatex and Donation remain the internal application and entity names.

External integrations

Mayar

QRIS tips

Mayar creates one dynamic QRIS transaction per tipped Note and confirms payment through the configured callback. It is the only payment provider.

Credentials needed

  • Mayar account and API key
  • Public application URL
  • Private webhook token
  • Configured Mayar callback

Data model

What the app needs to remember.

Note (stored internally as Donation)

idInternal identity.
senderRequired sender name, at most 64 characters.
reactionRequired for new Notes: bad, ok, good, or great; may be absent on legacy records.
messageOptional plain-text feedback, at most 280 characters.
tip amountOptional positive IDR amount; absent means feedback-only.
Mayar IDMayar transaction identity for tipped Notes; absent for free Notes.

Milestones

1

Reaction-Ready Tip Flow

Make reaction a first-class part of the current paid flow while keeping every legacy record usable.

What gets built

  • Current tip form requires exactly one Bad, Okay, Good, or Great selector with a representative emoji.
  • Missing/invalid reaction and sender over 64 characters show inline validation; optional message is limited to 280.
  • The selected reaction remains attached through QR creation, payment confirmation, admin display compatibility, and celebration.
  • Existing records without a reaction continue to render and operate safely.

Not in this milestone

  • No free submission or Sent state.
  • No floating emoji overlay.
  • No appreciation toggle, inbox filters, branding, or route change.
  • No custom or multiple reactions.
Done when: In a browser, a viewer cannot advance the existing tip flow without selecting one reaction, can complete QR checkout with one, and legacy reaction-less entries remain viewable without errors.
2

Free Notes with Safe Submission

Enable immediate payment-free feedback with spam protection from its first release.

What gets built

  • A viewer submits required sender and reaction plus optional message without payment.
  • An accepted free Note is Sent immediately, needs no transaction, and appears in admin with no amount.
  • Success shows a thank-you and resets the form.
  • After an accepted free Note, that IP receives a friendly 10-second cooldown; rejected attempts preserve entered fields.
  • Tipped submissions remain exempt, and summaries safely handle absent amounts.

Not in this milestone

  • No overlay broadcast or animation yet.
  • No optional-appreciation collapsed experience yet.
  • No admin filters or Notable terminology sweep.
  • No CAPTCHA, bans, fingerprinting, filtering, configuration, or IP display.
Done when: In a browser, a viewer can send free feedback and see a reset thank-you, the streamer sees it in admin without an amount, and a second accepted free send from the same IP within 10 seconds is blocked without losing fields while the tipped path remains available.
3

Floating Overlay Reactions

Turn accepted free reactions into ambient, ephemeral feedback in the existing OBS source.

What gets built

  • Each accepted free Note displays one randomly chosen emoji from its pool: bad 😅 🫠 💤 🙈; okay 😐 🤔 😶 🫤; good 😊 👍 🙌 💪; great 🤩 🔥 🎉 ⭐.
  • Emoji appear at varied positions, follow organic varied paths, and disappear after 3–4 seconds.
  • Multiple free reactions display simultaneously and reveal neither sender nor message.
  • The same overlay retains terminal tip celebrations with sender, amount, message, sound, confetti, sequencing, recovery, and replay.
  • A tipped Note never also floats; its stored reaction does not alter the celebration.

Not in this milestone

  • No recovery or replay for free reactions.
  • No separate overlay route or queue for floating reactions.
  • No custom emoji, movement controls, or themes.
  • No appreciation-form redesign, admin filters, or branding sweep.
Done when: With /overlay visible in a browser, several free Notes produce simultaneous, varied emoji-only floats that vanish within 3–4 seconds, while a confirmed tip produces only the existing queued celebration and a reload does not recover free reactions.
4

Optional Appreciation Experience

Make free feedback the obvious default and payment a deliberate optional act of appreciation.

What gets built

  • The mobile-first form shows sender, one reaction, optional message, and free submission by default.
  • A collapsed/off appreciation toggle reveals the current amount presets and custom amount.
  • Tipped submission shows sender, amount, QR, instructions, and expiry, with a way back.
  • QR creation errors are clear and retryable without discarding the Note.
  • Only confirmed payments celebrate; abandoned QR transactions remain pending.

Not in this milestone

  • No admin inbox redesign or route/branding sweep.
  • No extra payment methods, receipts, payment history, refunds, or auto-cancellation.
  • No manual-paid action or provider change.
  • No anonymous, rich-content, edit, or history flow.
Done when: On a mobile browser, free feedback is the default path; enabling appreciation reveals existing amount choices, reaches a complete QR screen, supports going back and retrying an error, and does not celebrate before confirmation.
5

Unified Admin Inbox

Give the streamer one operational inbox for feedback and appreciation tips.

What gets built

  • All is the default, with Tips and Feedback filters.
  • Newest-first cards show sender, reaction, optional message, time, type, status, and amount only for tips.
  • Paid tips alone offer replay; free and pending Notes do not.
  • New free feedback and payment updates appear live.
  • Empty states and operational summaries work when amounts are absent.

Not in this milestone

  • No edits, deletes, moderation, search, export, analytics, IP data, or bulk actions.
  • No replay for feedback or pending tips.
  • No expanded reporting.
  • No public route or full branding change yet.
Done when: In the authenticated admin browser, All/Tips/Feedback produce correct newest-first cards, incoming changes appear live, nil amounts render safely, and replay is visible and functional only on paid tips.
6

Notable Branding and Routing

Complete the narrative pivot across every user-facing surface without disruptive internal renaming.

What gets built

  • Public, payment, overlay, and admin copy uses Notable, Note/feedback, Sender, and Tip/appreciation.
  • The cyan-purple terminal visual style is retained.
  • The canonical public experience is at / and /donate redirects to /.
  • Public sender names and messages remain private to the sender flow and admin, except tip celebration content.

Not in this milestone

  • No Donatex application or Donation entity rename.
  • No logo package, custom domain, marketing site, or localization.
  • No visual theme replacement.
  • No new product functionality beyond copy and routing.
Done when: Browsing /, the QR flow, /overlay, and admin shows consistent Notable terminology and retained styling; visiting /donate redirects to /; no user-facing donation-first copy remains.
7

End-to-End Refinement and Release Check

Polish the complete Notable journey and prove the pivot is ready without expanding scope.

What gets built

  • Validation, cooldown, thank-you, empty, QR error, payment, and recovery states are coherent and accessible.
  • The complete free and tipped paths are refined for mobile viewers.
  • The unified overlay is verified for OBS transparency, concurrent floats, sequential celebrations, restart recovery, and replay.
  • Mayar confirmation remains deduplicated, rejects mismatches, persists before broadcast, and celebrates only confirmed tips.
  • Complete regression and quality checks cover the approved experience.

Not in this milestone

  • No new features or broad redesign.
  • No additions from the v1 exclusion list.
  • No payment lifecycle expansion such as cancellation, refunds, receipts, history, or manual-paid.
  • No free-reaction persistence or recovery.
Done when: A reviewer can complete free and tipped journeys on mobile, observe correct simultaneous feedback and confirmed-only tip behavior in an OBS-sized browser, restart/replay paid alerts safely, and all regression, ExUnit/PhoenixTest, Credo, and Dialyxir checks pass.
